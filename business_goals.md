# Reachly Business Goals

## North star

Build Rohit's own independent Reachly: a content and publishing assistant that
founders, creators, and businesses can use with their own brand and accounts.

## Priorities

1. Establish one personal source of truth for ownership, docs, and development.
2. Keep useful copied generation and publishing capabilities while removing
   mandatory external-company identity, branding, and operational assumptions.
3. Make business setup simple: audience, goals, voice, approved facts, platform
   accounts, schedule, and provider keys.
4. Make generation and review reliable before turning on automated publishing.
5. Prepare independent hosted onboarding and billing, then choose and verify
   the personal deployment target.

## Near-term work

- Keep personal login, branding, deployment, and account ownership independent.
- Maintain business-specific inputs across standalone, hosted and generation API paths.
- Retain upgraded generation, research, scripts, ideas and performance inputs
  alongside the personal asset library, publishing and video capabilities.
- Validate one selected personal platform/account at a time with explicit posting
  authorization and actual publication evidence.
- Keep account data, context, assets, and history isolated.

## Operating principles

Use customer-owned provider keys and platform accounts. Do not import another
business's goals, scheduled posts, browser sessions, or account credentials.
Prefer APIs where available; browser failures must be visible and auditable.
Keep secrets out of source control and session notes.

Initial business workspaces are Council of AI and Council Network. Each has its
own profile, inputs, credentials, drafts, schedule, publishing history and metrics.
No fixed posting quota or live platform status has been established for this
personal restart. Historical schedules and deployments are not current goals.
See `docs/PERSONAL_REACHLY_STATUS.md` for source-level findings and pending work.
